## 1. What the paper says — and what it does not say

Yang 2025 §4.5.5 (`refs/text/yang2025_hybrid_boolean.txt:718-732`, Fig. 16 `:752-758`) reads verbatim:

> *"4.5.5 Handling coplanarity. When conducting Boolean operations on B-Rep models, the coplanarity between the surfaces of the two models is a commonly encountered degenerative case. As our discretization method does not maintain coplanarity in triangle meshes because of floating-point error introduced in discretization, it may lead to incorrect Boolean operation results. Therefore, it is necessary to check coplanar planes and perform 2D Boolean operations before mesh discretizations. Two coplanar planes will be segmented into three parts after a Boolean operation in 2D, as illustrated in Fig. 16. The overlapping part is replaced by a trimmed common planar surface, and identical meshes are generated for both models in this part. The boundaries of the common surface are regarded as intersection curves between the two models, and thus the Boolean operations can be conducted."*
>
> Fig. 16 caption (`:752-758`): *"Fig. 16. The top surfaces of the two models in (a) are coplanar. (b) is a 2D schematic of the top surfaces. In (c), the intersection points of the 2D curves are calculated, and a 2D Boolean operation is performed, segmenting two surfaces into three parts. In (d), the intersection part is replaced by one trimmed common planar surface for both models, and identical meshes are generated in this part. The common part and the other two parts share identical sampling points on their boundaries."*

### Explicit paper scope vs. physical hazard

1. **The paper names only planes.** Every noun in §4.5.5 (`:718-732`), Fig. 16 (`:752-760`), and §5.5 (`:956-966, :1027-1033`) is planar: *"coplanar planes"*, *"trimmed common planar surface"*. Note that an earlier internal planning doc (`docs/yang_coincident_cylinder_stage0_plan.md:19-20`) misread §5.5 Fig. 24 as sharing cylindrical walls; in the paper (`:962-966, :1031-1033`), Fig. 24(b) unions 24 rotated elliptical cylinders that share **coplanar planar top and bottom caps**, not coincident cylindrical laterals.
2. **The paper's stated reason applies verbatim — and more strongly — to two faces on the same cylinder.** On a cylinder of radius $r$, Stage-1 discretization inscribes an $N$-gon prism whose chords sit radially inside the analytic cylinder by up to the sagitta $s = r(1 - \cos(\pi/N)) \le d_\varepsilon$. Even on bit-identical `Surface::Cylinder` parameters:
   - different $N$ (sized from each solid's own bbox/edges) place facets on two distinct inscribed prisms that cross radially;
   - different seam azimuths $\varphi_0$ rotate one inscribed $N$-gon through the other, producing $2N$ longitudinal sliver intersections;
   - non-conformal axial spans (e.g. a bore wall spanning $z \in [-5, 5]$ against a coaxial plug spanning $z \in [-2, 2]$) leave tall outer triangles spanning across the partner's cap-rim ring without sharing its vertices — discriminated in `crates/cherchi-rs/tests/task28_plug_in_bore.rs` as non-watertight on **both** native `cherchi-rs` and the upstream C++ Cherchi `mesh_booleans` reference;
   - opposing stored circle normals ($\pm\hat{a}$) evaluate `cos`/`sin` at $2\pi - \theta$ vs. $\theta$ (differing by $1\text{--}16\text{ ULPs} \approx 2\times 10^{-15}$ in IEEE-754 `f64`) and reverse the quad diagonal split across every column of the overlap band.

Extending §4.5.5 from coplanar planes to coincident cylinders is therefore a **co-surface extension beyond the paper's text**, motivated by the exact discretization failure §4.5.5 states (`:719-721`). Under governance A15.7, it must be ledgered explicitly (**N103**, `PROPOSED`) and cannot be self-signed `PERMANENT`.

## 2. Architecture of the coincident-cylinder subsystem

Unlike the planar Stage-0 pipeline (which is unified around `weld_coplanar_planes` + `stage0_preprocess`), coincident-cylinder handling was built across four historical increments (PR-5 membrane detector, PR-6 rim-arrangement weld, M8-cyl Increment 1 conformal outer mesh, and N4 azimuth-arc provenance `specs/n4_coincident_cylinder_provenance.md`) and spans five pipeline sites:

1. **Stage-0 dispatch (`crates/yang-rs/src/boolean.rs:1195-1209`):** `stage0::coincident_cylinder_stage0(a, b)` runs **only** when planar `stage0::stage0_preprocess(a, b)?` returns `None`.
2. **Group detection & conformal re-tessellation (`crates/yang-rs/src/stage0/cylinder.rs:260-1139`):** `detect_coincident_cylinder_groups`, `coincident_cylinder_stage0`, `cluster_rim_rings`, `build_conformal_outer_mesh`, `band_strip`, and N4 provenance helpers (`smallest_covering_arc`, `arc_contains`, `cluster_face_arcs`).
3. **Unconditional pair scan (`crates/yang-rs/src/boolean.rs:1220`, `stage0/cylinder.rs:46-104`):** `detect_coincident_cylinder_pairs(a, b)` emits `Vec<PairCylinder>` regardless of whether planar `stage0_preprocess` or `coincident_cylinder_stage0` ran.
4. **Post-arrangement weld & membrane rule (`crates/yang-rs/src/boolean.rs:2072-2144, 2339-2362`):** PR-6 welds sub-`TAU_WORK` duplicate arrangement vertices lying within `p.band` of any `PairCylinder`; the post-`keep_set` membrane filter matches multi-label sheet centroids against `cyl_pairs` via `centroid_on_cylinder` (`boolean/predicates.rs:136-150`) within `max(d_ε(a), d_ε(b), p.band)` and keeps or drops the sheet by `(BoolOp, opposite)`.
5. **Downstream identical-surface guards:**
   - Stage-2 shell contact (`crates/yang-rs/src/shell_contact.rs:297-313`, matrix `4.2-11`) skips identical-`Surface` pairs.
   - Stage-3 SSI (`crates/yang-rs/src/stage3_ssi.rs:1037-1056`) calls `cylinders_are_coincident(surf0, surf1, tol)` (`boolean/predicates.rs:156-195`) to skip `ssi_rs::intersect` on coincident `Cylinder × Cylinder` pairs (which would return `DegenerateInput`).
   - Stage-4 patch merge (`crates/yang-rs/src/stage4_correct.rs:485-513`) includes a `Surface::Cylinder` arm in `merge_same_plane_patches`'s `SurfKey`.

## 3. Step-by-step comparison: Yang §4.5.5 (5 steps) vs. Planar Stage 0 vs. Cylinder Stage 0

### Step 1 — Pre-discretization coincidence detection (`:722-724`)

- **Paper:** Check coplanar surfaces before mesh discretization (`:722-724`), under uniform model tolerances (`:743-745`: distance $d_p = 10^{-7}$, angle $10^{-6}\text{ rad}$).
- **Planar path:**
  1. `stage0/plane_weld.rs:52` (`weld_coplanar_planes`, called at `boolean.rs:1110`, ledger **N79**) rewrites B's `Surface::Plane`, curved-edge centers/vertices, and loop vertices onto each plane group's canonical A-face plane whenever `0 < gap <= band / 100.0`.
  2. `boolean/coplanar_scan.rs:140` (`scan_near_coplanar`) tests plane offset `gap <= band` (`:247`), extent-scaled normal parallelism `sin * extent <= band` (`:263`), and 3D `band`-inflated AABB overlap (`:289, :320`).
  3. Pairs in the sub-resolution gap window `band / 100.0 < gap <= band` trigger a loud `Err(YangError::SubResolutionCoplanarGap)` (`stage0/mod.rs:350`, ledger **N57**); intra-solid near-coplanar pairs trigger `Err(YangError::CoplanarFacesUnsupported)` (`stage0/mod.rs:305`).
- **Cylinder path & how/why it differs:**
  1. **Runs before Stage 2, but probes Stage 1 first (`cylinder.rs:509-548`):** Like planar Stage 0 (which calls `stage1_tessellate` inside `disc_rim_ring` / `face_polygon_2d_tessellated` to obtain curved chord rings), `coincident_cylinder_stage0` runs before Cherchi Stage 2 and calls `stage1_tessellate` twice per operand — once (`probe0_a`, `probe0_b`) to discover each cluster's natural ring count $N$, and once (`tess_a`, `tess_b`) with `min_n_seg = Some(shared_n)`.
  2. **Preempted by planar Stage 0 (`boolean.rs:1206-1209`):** Because `coincident_cylinder_stage0` sits in the `None =>` arm of `stage0_preprocess(a, b)?`, **any boolean that has both a near-coplanar plane pair and a coincident cylinder pair completely skips `coincident_cylinder_stage0`**. (For instance, a plug flush with one end cap of a bore triggers `stage0_preprocess` on the planar cap pair, leaving the cylinder walls to raw Stage-1 meshes + `detect_coincident_cylinder_pairs`.)
  3. **No B-Rep cylinder weld (N79 analog missing):** There is no `weld_coincident_cylinders` rewriting B's `Surface::Cylinder` (`axis_point`, `axis_dir`, `radius`), rim `Curve::Circle` centers/radii, or seam vertices onto A's canonical cylinder. Near-coincident cylinders within `band` retain distinct analytic parameters in B-Rep and distinct Stage-1 outer/cap rim radii.
  4. **Unscaled angular test (`cylinder.rs:186`, `predicates.rs:181`):** `cylinders_coincident` compares dimensionless `sin = ‖ad_a × ad_b‖` directly against `band.max(TAU_MODEL)` rather than `sin * extent <= band` (`coplanar_scan.rs:263`). Over an axial length $L \gg 1$, an axis tilt $\le \text{band}$ rad can displace the surface by $L \cdot \sin\theta > \text{band}$.
  5. **No `#178` sub-resolution gap STOP or intra-solid scan (N57 gap):** `cylinders_coincident` accepts `|r_a - r_b| <= band` and `perp_dist <= band` up to the full `band` without stopping on `(band / 100.0, band]` (already noted under N57 at `docs/yang_deviations.md:3695-3700`), and `cluster_cylinder_faces` (`cylinder.rs:334`) merges same-sense intra-solid cylinders within `band` without an intra-solid sub-resolution STOP.
  6. **Defect 0 — Unprojected local-frame axial extents (`cylinder.rs:75-78, 146, 299-311, 358-360, 405, 491-508`) and zero-area cap-touch pairs:** Planar Stage 0 projects both operands into one canonical plane frame (`stage0/frame.rs`). By contrast, `cylinder_faces` (`:146`) and `cylinder_faces_indexed` (`:405`) compute each face's axial interval `extent = [lo, hi]` relative to **that face's own `(axis_point, axis_dir)`**, and `detect_coincident_cylinder_pairs` (`:75-78`), `cluster_cylinder_faces` (`:358-360`), `detect_coincident_cylinder_groups` (`:299-311`), and `coincident_cylinder_stage0` (`:491-508`) compare `ca.extent` directly against `cb.extent` without transforming `cb.extent` into `(ca.axis_point, ca.axis_dir)` via $t_a = (\text{ap}_b - \text{ap}_a)\cdot\text{au}_a + (\text{au}_b\cdot\text{au}_a)\,t_b$. Consequently:
     - two **disjoint** coaxial cylinders extruded from separate sketch planes (`z ∈ [0, 5]` with `ap_a = (0,0,0)` and `z ∈ [10, 15]` with `ap_b = (0,0,10)`) both carry local `extent = [0, 5]` and falsely match as overlapping coincident cylinders;
     - a **strictly contained** revolved washer (`ap = (0,0,0)`, `extent = [-2, 2]`) inside an extruded shaft (`ap = (0,0,-5)`, `extent = [0, 10]`), or a `-Z`-extruded plug inside a `+Z`-extruded bore, is falsely rejected as `"partial overlap"` because `[0, 10]` does not contain `[-2, 2]` numerically before frame alignment;
     - even after frame alignment, `lo_a <= hi_b + band && lo_b <= hi_a + band` (`:77`) admits **zero-area cap-to-cap touches** (`hi_a == lo_b`, e.g. two cylinders stacked end-to-end along their planar cap), emitting a `PairCylinder` and activating PR-6's post-arrangement rim weld (`boolean.rs:2072`) when the two cylinders share only a 1D circle and zero 2D cylindrical overlap.

### Step 2 — 2D Boolean overlay in the cylinder's $(u, v)$ domain with periodic seam (`:724-725`)

- **Paper:** Intersect the 2D boundary curves in the shared surface's parameter domain and perform a 2D Boolean operation (`:724-725`). On a cylinder of radius $r$, the natural isometric domain is $(u, v) = (r\theta, z)$ on $(\mathbb{R} / 2\pi r\mathbb{Z}) \times \mathbb{R}$ with periodic seam at $\theta \equiv 0 \pmod{2\pi}$.
- **Planar path:** Projects loops to canonical 2D $(u, v)$ (`stage0/frame.rs`), runs an exact rational (`dashu::rational::RBig`) segment arrangement and trapezoidal sweep (`coplanar_overlay.rs:368, 386`) or exact `RBig` convex containment (`stage0/disc_pair.rs:71`), mints exact rational/analytic crossings onto `Circle` / `Ellipse` / `SurfacePair` rims (`stage0/rim_chords.rs:359`), and groups arbitrary multi-face components via `stage0/nary.rs:53, 166`. Unhandled planar configurations fail loudly with `Err(YangError::CoplanarFacesUnsupported)` (`stage0/mod.rs:360-383`, ledger **N17**).
- **Cylinder path & how/why it differs:**
  - **No general $(u, v) = (r\theta, z)$ 2D overlay is implemented.** Instead, `coincident_cylinder_stage0` (`cylinder.rs:470-505, 581-591`) restricts admission to the **1D axial-containment special case**:
    1. `groups.len() == 1` (`:470`, single shared cylinder; multi-cylinder ops excluded);
    2. homogeneous `reversed` per solid (`:358`) and `g.opposite == true` across solids (`:481`, bore cavity vs. solid shaft only; same-sense coaxial unions/subtracts excluded);
    3. full-$2\pi$ walls on both operands yielding exactly 2 equal-size rim rings per cluster (`outer_rings.len() == 2 && cont_rings.len() == 2`, `:582`, `:726`) — partial-$\theta$ arc patches ($< 2\pi$), cross-bored/holed cylinder walls, and oblique elliptical-rim cylinders are excluded;
    4. axial containment within `g.band`: `lo_a <= lo_b + g.band && hi_b <= hi_a + g.band` or vice versa (`:493-505`) — partial axial overlaps (`lo_a < lo_b < hi_a < hi_b`) are excluded.
  - **How the periodic $2\pi$ seam is handled:** Because both walls span the full circle $[0, 2\pi)$ bounded by two constant-$z$ rings, the 2D Boolean on $(\mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z}) \times \mathbb{R}$ collapses to a product $[0, 2\pi) \times [z_0, z_1]$. Periodic wrap-around is handled without cutting open a chart by sorting ring vertices by global azimuth `atan2(y, x).rem_euclid(2π)` in `ortho_basis(axis_dir)` (`cylinder.rs:687-696, 1026-1040`) and closing the ring strip from `k = n - 1` back to `kn = 0` (`:1058-1071`). For multi-arc full-$2\pi$ clusters (such as `err.waffle`'s 4-arc-patch gear bore), `smallest_covering_arc` and `arc_contains` (`:762-798`) handle arcs straddling $\theta = 0 / 2\pi$ via `end < start` wrap-around.
  - **Silent `Ok(None)` fall-through vs. planar loud STOP:** Every out-of-scope cylinder case returns `Ok(None)` (`:440-443` *"LOUD-free fall-through"*), falling back to un-preprocessed Stage-1 meshes and relying on downstream `FaceResolutionFailed` / `NonManifoldOutput` gates if the raw arrangement fails, whereas planar Stage 0 refuses unhandled coplanar pairs upfront with `CoplanarFacesUnsupported` (**N17**).

### Step 3 — Three-way segmentation (`A-only`, `B-only`, `Overlap`) (`:725-726`, Fig. 16c)

- **Paper:** Segment the two surfaces into three parts: $A \setminus B$, $B \setminus A$, and $A \cap B$ (`:725-726`, Fig. 16c).
- **Planar path:** Exact rational cell classification into `RegionClass::AOnly`, `RegionClass::BOnly`, and `RegionClass::Overlap`, guarded by exact rational area conservation `area(AOnly) + area(Overlap) == area(A)` and `area(BOnly) + area(Overlap) == area(B)` (`coplanar_overlay.rs:74-90`).
- **Cylinder path & how/why it differs:** Under full-$\theta$ axial containment ($\text{cont} \subseteq \text{outer}$), the three parts reduce to 1D axial $z$-bands:
  - $\text{cont}\text{-only} = \emptyset$;
  - $\text{Overlap} = [\text{cont\_rings}[0].z,\ \text{cont\_rings}[1].z]$ (the entire contained cylindrical wall);
  - $\text{outer}\text{-only} = [z_{\text{lo}},\ \text{cont\_rings}[0].z] \cup [\text{cont\_rings}[1].z,\ z_{\text{hi}}]$, omitting either outer band when `cont_rings` lies within `g.band` of $z_{\text{lo}}$ or $z_{\text{hi}}$ (`cylinder.rs:893`). No rational or surface-area conservation postcondition is checked.

### Step 4 — Common trimmed surface & identical overlap meshes (`:726-728`, Fig. 16d `:759-760`)

- **Paper:** *"The overlapping part is replaced by a trimmed common planar surface, and identical meshes are generated for both models in this part"* (`:726-728`), *"shar[ing] identical sampling points on their boundaries"* (`:759-760`).
- **Planar path:** Neither path materializes a pre-boolean B-Rep face (`4.5.5-06`). Planar Stage 0 snaps both faces onto the canonical plane (`plane_weld.rs:52`, `stage0/mod.rs:394-439`), emits `tris_for(&[AOnly, Overlap], false)` and `tris_for(&[BOnly, Overlap], opposite)` from **one shared triangulation** (`stage0/mod.rs:2168-2172`), and propagates boundary splits into adjacent faces and cylinder/cone/torus laterals via `SplitMap` and `RimSplitMap` (`stage0/mesh_build.rs:528`, `stage0/rim_chords.rs`).
- **Cylinder path & how/why it differs:**
  1. **Shared circumferential segment count $N$:** `coincident_cylinder_stage0` re-tessellates both solids via `stage1_tessellate_with_standing_overrides(..., Some(shared_n))` (`cylinder.rs:521-555`), where `shared_n = na.max(nb).max(a.forced_rim_n).max(b.forced_rim_n)` (raising $N$ shrinks chord sagitta and stays chord-valid).
  2. **Literal vertex copy for interior overlap boundaries (`cylinder.rs:907-913`):** `build_conformal_outer_mesh` clones `outer_tess.verts` and appends literal `Point3` copies of `cont_tess`'s interior `cont_rings` vertices, then triangulates consecutive $z$-rings of `outer_mesh` with `band_strip` (`:974-993`).
  3. **Provenance (`N4`, `cylinder.rs:762-841, 955-965`):** `Stage0.pairs` is left empty (`:647`); `outer_tri_face` maps each `band_strip` column's midpoint azimuth to the covering arc-patch face (`u32::MAX` sentinel fallback).
  4. **Defects where `coincident_cylinder_stage0` returns `Ok(Some(_))` WITHOUT generating identical overlap meshes (diverging from both §4.5.5 `:727-728` and planar `tris_for`):**
     - **Defect A — One-sided `band_strip` rebuild leaves crossed quad diagonals (`cylinder.rs:611` vs. `:975, :1073`):** `outer_mesh`'s overlap band is built by `band_strip`, which sorts both rings CCW around `g.axis_dir` and splits every quad `(b0, b1, t1, t0)` along diagonal `b0—t1` (`lo_ccw[k] — hi_ccw[k+1]`). Meanwhile `cont_mesh` was NOT rebuilt by `band_strip` — line `611` kept `cont_tess.tris` verbatim! Whenever `cont`'s top rim edge has stored circle/arc normal opposing `g.axis_dir` — which occurs systematically on **every revolved bore washer** (`kernel-v2/src/construct/revolve.rs:793`, inner ring `nu = neg(normal)`) and **every `ArcPolygon`-extruded hole bore** (`kernel-v2/src/construct/extrude.rs:618, 709`, CW hole `wb_normal = neg(a)`) when the bored body is axially contained in a taller shaft — `tessellate_lateral_face` (`stage1_tessellate.rs:4875-4886, 4928-4938`) walks `top_ring` CW around `g.axis_dir` and splits every quad along the **opposite diagonal `b1—t0` (`lo_ccw[k+1] — hi_ccw[k]`)**. Furthermore, because `ortho_basis(-Z)` vs. `ortho_basis(+Z)` samples $2\pi(N-k)/N$ vs. $2\pi k/N$, `cos`/`sin` differ by up to $16\text{ ULPs}$ ($\approx 2\times 10^{-15}$), making every quad non-planar (`orient3d != 0`) so the two crossed diagonals are **skew in 3D**: zero triangles pocket-dedup into a multi-label membrane, `boolean.rs:2339` never fires on the overlap, and the boolean either fails with `NonManifoldOutput` / `NonManifoldInput` or traps $N$ sub-ULP sliver cavities!
     - **Defect B — Equal-extent and one-end-flush ring skip (`cylinder.rs:893, 921-924`):** When a `cont_ring` lies within `g.band` of $z_{\text{lo}}$ or $z_{\text{hi}}$ (without a coplanar planar cap preempting Stage 0 — e.g., a cone-chamfered bore whose cylindrical section shares one or both $z$-bounds with a plug), `build_conformal_outer_mesh` skips inserting `cont_ring` (`r.z <= z_lo + band || r.z >= z_hi - band`) and, when both ends are flush (`all.len() == outer_rings.len()`), returns `outer_tess.tris` unchanged! If `outer_rings` and `cont_rings` at that flush $z$-level are not already bit-identical in `Point3` coordinates (e.g. $2\times 10^{-15}$ opposite-normal trig drift, sub-`band` radius/axis offset, or seam phase shift), `outer_mesh` uses `outer_rings`'s vertices while `cont_mesh` uses `cont_rings`'s vertices — again emitting **non-identical overlap meshes** while claiming `Ok(Some(_))`! (And even when their flush ring vertices *are* bit-identical, returning `outer_tess.tris` and `cont_tess.tris` unchanged on equal-extent leaves Defect A's crossed diagonals unfixed.)
     - **Limitation C — Azimuth phase alignment (`cylinder.rs:1041-1047`):** Because `coincident_cylinder_stage0` does not inject `rim_overrides` into the operands' caps, `band_strip` requires sorted ring azimuths to align index-for-index within `tol = 0.25 * (2π / n)`. Rotating a coaxial plug by $\pi / N$ (half a facet) causes `band_strip` to return `None` (`Ok(None)` fall-through to non-watertight Stage-1 meshes). Moreover, if rotated by a nonzero angle $\Delta\varphi \in (0, 0.25 \cdot 2\pi/N]$ on an interior containment case, the interior overlap band uses `cont_rings` on both meshes (identical once Defect A is fixed), while the outer bands $[z_{\text{lo}}, \text{ov\_lo}]$ and $[\text{ov\_hi}, z_{\text{hi}}]$ connect `outer_rings` to `cont_rings` across $\Delta\varphi$; at a flush end ($\text{ov\_lo} \approx z_{\text{lo}}$), any $\Delta\varphi \ne 0$ makes the flush ring vertices distinct, so claiming `Ok(Some(_))` there violates §4.5.5 unless the flush rings are bit-identical.

### Step 5 — Overlap boundaries regarded as intersection curves (`:728-731`)

- **Paper:** *"The boundaries of the common surface are regarded as intersection curves between the two models, and thus the Boolean operations can be conducted"* (`:728-731`).
- **Planar path:** Identical `Overlap` triangles collapse in `cherchi-rs` input prep into multi-label cells; `boolean.rs:2280-2362` keeps or drops the multi-label membrane by `(BoolOp, opposite)` using `PairPlane` + `la.source` provenance; surviving `Plane × Plane` edges take `Curve::LineSegment` (`stage3_ssi.rs:1032-1035`) and `Plane × Curved` edges run `ssi_rs::intersect`.
- **Cylinder path & how/why it differs:**
  - When the overlap band's triangles are bit-identical, `cherchi-rs` pocket-dedups them into one multi-label cylinder sheet, and PR-6 (`boolean.rs:2072-2144`) fuses sub-`TAU_WORK` arrangement duplicates on the cylinder rim.
  - `boolean.rs:2339-2362` resolves multi-label cylinder sheets via `cyl_pairs`:
    - **Dropped membrane** (e.g. `Union` or `Intersect` with `opposite == true`, or `Subtract` with `opposite == false`): along the overlap boundary $z = \text{ov\_lo}, \text{ov\_hi}$, `outer`'s surviving `Surface::Cylinder` band (`InputId::A`) meets `cont`'s surviving cap `Surface::Plane` / `Cone` (`InputId::B`). Stage 3 (`stage3_ssi.rs:1095`) runs `ssi_rs::intersect` on `(Cylinder, Plane)` and types those boundary rings as exact analytic `Curve::Circle` intersection curves.
    - **Kept membrane** (e.g. `Subtract` with `opposite == true`, or `Union` / `Intersect` with `opposite == false`): adjacent `Cylinder × Cylinder` patches on the shared cylinder hit `stage3_ssi.rs:1037-1056` (`cylinders_are_coincident`), skipping `ssi_rs::intersect` (which refuses identical quadrics with `DegenerateInput`) and leaving the seam chords as `Curve::LineSegment` so Stage-4 relocation does not pull chord vertices radially across adjacent membrane triangles.
  - Note: unlike planar `boolean.rs:2304-2324` (which disambiguates mixed-orientation n-ary groups via `la.source` → `tri_face` against `(p.face_a, p.face_b)`), `PairCylinder` carries no face indices and `boolean.rs:2339` takes `cyl_pairs.iter().find(...)` by centroid radial distance $\le \max(d_\varepsilon(a), d_\varepsilon(b), p.\text{band})$.

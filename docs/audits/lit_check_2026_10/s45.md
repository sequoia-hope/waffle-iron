### Justifications for status changes and retained `FAITHFUL` grades

#### 1. `4.5-02`: `FAITHFUL` → `PARTIAL + ORDER`
- **Paper (`refs/text/yang2025_hybrid_boolean.txt:654-656`):** *"Our method first detects failure regions and then employs two strategies to solve the problem."*
- **Code (`stage4_correct.rs:2546, 4710, 6382, 6395, 6402`, `stage4_correct/s451.rs:89, 530`, `boolean/failures.rs:1304`, `boolean.rs:622, 935`):**
  1. Around 47 `LocalRefinementRequired` gates across `stage4_correct.rs` (`41`), `stage4_relocate.rs` (`4`), `stage4_correct/reversal.rs` (`1`), and `stage4_boundary_curve.rs` (`1`) return `Err` on the first failing vertex before `s451_plan_repairs` (`stage4_correct/s451.rs:530`) ever runs (ledger `N84`).
  2. `solve_crease_transit` (`stage4_boundary_curve.rs:1643`, called at `stage4_correct.rs:2546`) mutates the mesh inline during the triple relocation loop *before* failure collection (ledger `N87`).
  3. `corner_transit_apply` (`stage4_correct/corner_transit.rs:4`, called at `stage4_correct.rs:6382`) and the §4-I9 / T2 domain detectors (`stage4_correct.rs:6395, 6402`) run *after* §4.5.3 reversal correction (`:5089`) and §4.4.1 `update_patches` (`:5384`).
  4. Strategy 2 (`refine_loop`, `boolean.rs:622, 935`) runs after Stage-6 B-Rep emission, wrapping all of Stages 0–6 (ledger `N62`).

#### 2. `4.5-05`: `FAITHFUL` → `PARTIAL`
- **Paper (`refs/text/yang2025_hybrid_boolean.txt:659-661`):** *"Then for each erroneous region bounded by two successfully optimized points v0 and v1 on the same surface, i.e., all the optimization between v0 and v1 has failed, we apply the first strategy"*
- **Code (`stage4_correct/s451.rs:578-611, 698-751`):** `s451_plan_repairs` splits on `own_curves`:
  - If `own_curves.is_empty()` (`:584`, unattributed torus-carried vertices), `:607-611` requires `deg == 2 && bounds.len() == 2` on the immediate 1-hop curve neighbours, explicitly declining multi-vertex regions (`"region k > 1"` at `:609`).
  - Otherwise (`:698-751`), it requires `own_curves.len() == 1` with `c != Curve::LineSegment` (`:579`) and walks `curve_adj` up to `members.len() <= 128` (`:721`). `LineSegment` chains and `k > 1` torus runs cannot form an erroneous region (`SurfacePair` chains pass `:579` and form a bounded region here, declining later at the shared `Cone + Plane` check `s451.rs:783-789`, row `4.5.1-01`). Under the matrix's vocabulary (`PARTIAL` = *"the mechanism exists and covers a named subset"*), this is `PARTIAL` (ledger `N90`).

#### 3. `4.5-07`: `FAITHFUL` → `PARTIAL`
- **Paper (`refs/text/yang2025_hybrid_boolean.txt:659-667`):** *"Then for each erroneous region bounded by two successfully optimized points v0 and v1 on the same surface … we apply the first strategy … If such bound cannot be found or after crossing boundaries the optimization still fails, we increase the mesh resolution locally and re-optimize as the second strategy shown in Fig. 14."*
- **Code (`stage4_correct/s451.rs:561-967`, `stage4_correct.rs:4706-4758`, `boolean.rs:935`):**
  1. `s451_plan_repairs` processes `s45_failures` as an **all-or-nothing batch**: any single unrepairable vertex returns `Err(())`, causing `stage4_correct.rs:4747-4758` to discard all `Ok(plans)` already built for other bounded regions on the same sweep and abort Stage 4 into `refine_loop`.
  2. `"after crossing boundaries the optimization still fails"` is never attempted for interior regions because `s451_plan_repairs` declines immediately when the midpoint's step crosses a face crease (`stage4_correct/s451.rs:839-845`, ledger `N90`).

#### 4. `4.5-08`: `FAITHFUL` → `ORDER` (overlap with `3-02`)
- **Paper (`refs/text/yang2025_hybrid_boolean.txt:667-668`):** *"The above procedures are repeated if optimization failure persists."*
- **Code (`boolean.rs:702-793`):** `for iter in 0..REFINE_LOOP_ITER_CAP` repeats `failures::region` → `BRep::refined_by` → `boolean_once(&ra, &rb, op, backend)` (`:761`) → `failures::failures(&out)` (`:763`), re-refining `prev_region` on faceless refined STOPs (`:704-731`). Because this loop wraps the entire Stages 0–6 pipeline after B-Rep emission rather than iterating inside Stage 4's optimization phase before §4.4.1 mesh updating and §4.4.2 classification, its status is `ORDER`. **Note on overlap:** this is the exact same pipeline-order fact already graded `ORDER` in Slice 1 row `3-02` (`docs/audits/yang_2025_audit.md:332`) and slice inversion `(c)` — visibly one finding across both rows, not two separate defects.

#### 5. `4.5.1-02`: `FAITHFUL` → `PARTIAL`
- **Paper (`refs/text/yang2025_hybrid_boolean.txt:678-679`):** *"The points in the erroneous region are removed and replaced with the midpoint p0 of v0 and v1."*
- **Code (`stage4_correct/s451.rs:607-618, 690-695, 721-723, 783-795, 928-939`; `stage4_correct.rs:4721-4736`):** Multi-point removal (`collapse_vertex` of `plan.victims` onto `plan.survivor` with §4-I8 carrier containment at `s451.rs:928-939`) and midpoint replacement are implemented only for shared `Cone + Plane` chains (`≤ 128` vertices); the torus arm (`:607-611, :692`) declines `region k > 1` (`victims: Vec::new()`), and every other surface pair declines at `:783-789`.

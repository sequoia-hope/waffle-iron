## REVIEWER-CONFIRMED BUGS

1. **§4.5.3 all-open-conic loops skipped by `sweep_reversed_intersections` (`4.5.3-08`, `crates/yang-rs/src/stage4_correct/reversal.rs:109-133`).**
   - `any_intersection` (`reversal.rs:109-121`) matches only `Some(Curve::Circle) | Some(Curve::Ellipse) | Some(Curve::LineSegment) | Some(Curve::SurfacePair)`, omitting `Curve::Parabola` and `Curve::Hyperbola`, while `any_pair` (`:123-130`) requires `!curves.contains_key(&key)`. Consequently, an all-open-conic boundary loop — e.g., a prism with sides parallel to a cone's axis piercing its lateral face, producing a closed cycle of 4 `Hyperbola` arcs — fails `any_intersection || any_pair` (`:131-133`) and is **never reversal-corrected**. The 2026-10-24 `N89` item 3 `RESOLVED` closure was premature because its test pin (`tests_unit/i13_junction_overrun.rs::conic_param_and_reversal_cover_parabola_and_hyperbola`) calls the helper functions (`mixed_cycle_shared_conic`, `conic_param_deltas`) directly, not `sweep_reversed_intersections`.
2. **§4.5.5 `Curve::Ellipse` omitted from `face_plane` AABB expansion (`4.5.5-01`, `crates/yang-rs/src/boolean/coplanar_scan.rs:184-205`).**
   - `face_plane` expands loop AABBs only for `Curve::Circle` (`:191`) even though the immediately preceding comment (`:184`) explicitly states ``A `Circle`/`Ellipse` loop edge's endpoints are only its seam — the swept curve reaches much further``. Framing caveat: production `kernel-v2` ellipse loops are split into several minor arcs (`crates/kernel-v2/src/boolean/to_yang.rs:395-413`), so production loops are not 1-point boxes (only direct `yang-rs` 1-edge closed `Ellipse` loops are), **but** their AABB covers only the arc endpoints and misses the curved bulge, so an off-center coplanar partner overlapping only the bulge can be missed by `scan_near_coplanar` (`:320`).
- **Minor (`4.5.3-15`, `crates/yang-rs/src/stage4_correct/reversal.rs:1188`):** `if t2 > 0.0` in `conic_param_deltas`'s `Curve::SurfacePair` arm has zero angular rank floor, letting a near-tangent `SurfacePair` take its tangent direction from floating-point noise instead of applying the paper's `10^-6` rad tolerance.

### Justifications for all 10 status changes (verbatim paper quote + code cite + reason)

1. **`4.5.3-01`: `FAITHFUL → ORDER`**
   - **Paper (`refs/text/yang2025_hybrid_boolean.txt:680-689,718`):**
     > "If the mesh resolution is not sufficient to maintain a one-to-one mapping between the mesh intersection and the B-Rep intersection curves, the surface intersection may exhibit a reverse sequence of points after convergence, see inset. The reversal of the intersection curve leads to topology errors within the curve and in the updated mesh after the process described in Sec. 4.4.1."
   - **Code (`crates/yang-rs/src/stage4_correct.rs:5056-5089, 5384`; `crates/yang-rs/src/boolean.rs:2131`):** `sweep_reversed_intersections` (`stage4_correct.rs:5089`) runs before `update_patches` (`:5384`), **but** Stage 4 itself runs on the post-§4.4.2 classified submesh (`keep_set`, ledger N80 / O-1), and two §4.4.1-type mesh-updating passes execute **before** `sweep_reversed_intersections`: `trim_beyond_corner_phantoms` (`:5056`, N81) and `retriangulate_collapsed_fan_regions` (`:5074`, whose comment at `:5068` explicitly describes it as *"the §4.4.1 triangulation-update half"*, N58).
   - **Why:** Reversal correction does not run before all §4.4.1 mesh updates or before §4.4.2 classification (matching the slice's own `Inversions` bullet).

2. **`4.5.3-02`: `FAITHFUL → PARTIAL`**

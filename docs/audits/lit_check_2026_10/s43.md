  > *"For the cases where the intersection point is on the boundary curve, we restrict it to move along the curve."*
- **Code (`crates/yang-rs/src/stage4_relocate.rs:367-420`, `crates/yang-rs/src/stage4_correct.rs:2386-2555, :4496-4565`, `crates/yang-rs/src/stage4_boundary_curve.rs:44-78, :142-194`):**

Nothing restricts iterates or the converged root to the boundary curve's trimmed segment; stepping across another crease is detected only post-hoc by `crease_crossed_by_step` (`:2500`, repaired by non-paper `solve_crease_transit` under `N87`) or `boundary_domain_postcondition` (`:6402`, recording T2 for §4.5.2 under `N66`).

#### 2. `4.3-07`: `FAITHFUL` → `DEVIATES`
- **Paper (`refs/text/yang2025_hybrid_boolean.txt:1232-1250`, Appendix C):**
  > *"We treat the parametric coordinates (u_0, v_0) and (s_0, t_0) of the intersection point r_0 on the surfaces S_A = s_1(u, v) and S_B = s_2(s, t) as variables, and solve for the zero of D(u, v, s, t) = s_1(u, v) − s_2(s, t). ... The equation becomes: ∇D∇Dᵀ a_k = s_2(s_k, t_k) − s_1(u_k, v_k). The variables x = (u, v, s, t)ᵀ in each iteration are updated as x_{k+1} = x_k + ∇Dᵀ a_k."*
- **Code (`crates/yang-rs/src/stage4_relocate.rs:285-348` `relocate_onto_implicit_pair`):**
  - Evaluates two signed-distance implicit functions `(f0, n0) = surface_distance_and_normal(s0, x)` and `(f1, n1) = surface_distance_and_normal(s1, x)` at a **single 3D point `x ∈ ℝ³`** (`F: ℝ³ → ℝ²`), solves the `2×2` system `J Jᵀ y = f`, and updates `x[i] -= n0[i] * y0 + n1[i] * y1`.
- **Why `DEVIATES`:** Appendix C formulates `D: ℝ⁴ → ℝ³` on four surface parameters `x = (u, v, s, t)ᵀ` tracking two separate surface points `s_1(u_k, v_k)` and `s_2(s_k, t_k)` via the `3×3` system `∇D∇Dᵀ a_k = s_2 − s_1`. Our `relocate_onto_implicit_pair` instead solves `F: ℝ³ → ℝ²` on a single 3D point with two signed-distance residuals, whose minimum-norm step onto two linearized planes IS algebraically §4.3.2's geometric projection onto `P_A^k ∩ P_B^k` (`4.3-09`), not App. C's 4-parameter two-point Newton. That structural difference (`x ∈ ℝ³` with 2 residuals vs `x = (u,v,s,t)ᵀ` with `D: ℝ⁴ → ℝ³`) alone justifies `DEVIATES` (`N7`, `N91`, `D14`).

#### 3. `4.3-09`: `FAITHFUL` → `PARTIAL`
- **Paper (`refs/text/yang2025_hybrid_boolean.txt:493-500`):**
  > *"Similar to Newton's method that linearizes the equations, we linearize the two surfaces geometrically as two tangent planes P_A^k in (u_k, v_k) and P_B^k in (s_k, t_k), i.e., P_A^k(u, v) = S_A(u_k, v_k) + ∇S_A(u_k, v_k) · (u, v)ᵀ, and P_B^k(s, t) = S_B(s_k, t_k) + ∇S_B(s_k, t_k) · (s, t)ᵀ. Then we project the point r_1^k onto the intersection line L_k = P_A^k ∩ P_B^k to obtain the optimal parametric locations of the two points for the iteration step."*
- **Code (`crates/yang-rs/src/stage4_relocate.rs:285-351` `relocate_onto_implicit_pair`, `:1790-1837` `project_onto_cone_section`):**
  - Projects `x ∈ ℝ³` orthogonally onto the intersection line of the two linearized implicit tangent planes (`x - n0 * y0 - n1 * y1`), without maintaining `(u_k, v_k)` / `(s_k, t_k)` parametric coordinates (`D14`).
  - Used only for `Curve::SurfacePair` (`stage4_correct.rs:4348`), torus pairs (`:4507`), and `cone ∩ plane` sections (`:4015`, `N97`), whereas circles, cylinder-ellipses, and lines bypass §4.3.2 for closed-form projection (`4.3-13`, `N7`). Hence `PARTIAL`.

#### 4. `4.3-24`: `FAITHFUL` → `DEVIATES`
- **Paper (`refs/text/yang2025_hybrid_boolean.txt:586-592, :743-745`):**
  > *"If the following conditions are met: h < d_p × 10^2, l < d_p × 10^3, α < π/18, then no further subdivision is performed and the refinement between p and q terminates."* (`:586-592`)
  > *"The distance tolerance in our paper is uniformly set to d_p and is specified as 10^-7 in our experiments."* (`:743-745`)
- **Code (`crates/yang-rs/src/stage4_correct/collapse.rs:925-937, :958-963`):**

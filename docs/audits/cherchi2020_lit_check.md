### E — Exactness & soundness defects in geometric predicates (highest priority)

- **E-1 (`U-CH-1`, CRITICAL). `max_component_in_triangle_normal` (`predicates/orientation.rs:55-110`) has an unsound `f64` semi-static filter at both $\mathcal{O}(1)$ normal scale and subnormal scale, capable of selecting a projection plane in which the triangle has EXACT ZERO 2D AREA.** `max_component_filtered` (`:71-110`) sets `max_var = max(|a|, |b|, |c|)` on raw vertex coordinates (`:82-96`) instead of edge-difference magnitudes `max(|b - a|, |c - a|)` (`mesh_arrangement.txt:1020-1022`, `meyer_pion2008_fpg.txt:167-189`), uses `4.0 * f64::EPSILON = 8.881784197001252e-16` (`:97`, smaller than FPG's outward-rounded `8.88395e-16`), compares two noisy components via `az > ax + eps` (`:100-108`) without doubling `eps` or guarding `az > eps_valid`, and omits `SUBNORMAL_GUARD` (`eps == 0.0` below `~1.5e-154`). On an explicit $\mathcal{O}(1)$ vertical triangle (`max_var ≈ 0.84375`, exact $n_z \equiv 0$, exact dominant axis `Axis::X`), `max_component_in_triangle_normal` returns `Axis::Z` (`Plane::XY`), projecting the triangle to a collinear segment in `soup.rs:901`! (`C4.2-02`.)
- **E-2 (`U-CH-2`, HIGH). Three explicit-predicate modules call `geometry_predicates::orient2d` directly, bypassing `predicates::orient::orient2d`'s `N24` rational zero-certification.** Shewchuk §2.1 (`shewchukrobustr.txt:203-208`) excludes exponent underflow (`det` rounds to `0.0` below $2^{-1074}$). Ledger `N24` closed this in `predicates/orient.rs:79-93, 145-150`, but `predicates/collinearity.rs:30-32` (`points_are_collinear_3d`), `predicates/point_in_triangle.rs:97, 141-143` (`point_in_triangle_3d` / `point_in_triangle_2d`), and `predicates/simplex_location.rs:90` (`orient2d_sign`) call `geometry_predicates::orient2d` directly. On `(0,0,0), (1e-200,0,0), (0,1e-200,0)`, `points_are_collinear_3d` falsely returns `true`, causing `remove_degenerate_and_duplicated_triangles` (`arrangements/soup.rs:340`) to silently drop valid non-degenerate small-scale triangles. (`C5.1-01`, `SH2.1-01`, `SH4.3-03`.)
- **E-3 (`U-CH-3`, MEDIUM-HIGH). Plain-`f64` division ray-casting (`point_in_polygon`) and silent `< 1.79e-43` coordinate flushing (`spade_point`) remain live in `triangulation/mod.rs`.** `point_in_polygon` (`triangulation/mod.rs:1267-1285`) averages `(a+b+c)/3.0` in `f64` and runs `(xj - xi) * (py - yi) / (yj - yi) + xi` in `f64`; it is still called by `cdt_polygon_with_holes` (`:248-259`, both outer loop and holes) and by `cdt_polygon_with_holes_refined` (`:960-968`, holes — whereas `cdt_polygon_with_holes_refined_seeded` `:1153` was upgraded to `centroid_in_polygon_exact`). Separately, `spade_point` (`:1245-1254`) flushes `|x| < spade::MIN_ALLOWED_VALUE` (`1.793662034335766e-43`) to `0.0` while `centroid_in_polygon_exact` evaluates containment on unflushed coordinates, and `centroid_in_polygon_filtered` (`:1335`) lacks `+ SUBNORMAL_GUARD` on `ecy`. (`SH2.1-01`, `SH4.2-03`, `SH4.3-04`.)
- **E-4 (`U-CH-4`, MEDIUM). `point_in_triangle_3d` (`predicates/point_in_triangle.rs:70-121`) and `point_in_triangle_3d_loc` (`predicates/simplex_location.rs:133-173`) omit a 3D coplanarity (`orient3d == 0`) check, contradicting `point_in_triangle.rs`'s docstring.** Both functions project onto XY/YZ/ZX and skip zero-area projections, returning `StrictlyInside` for off-plane 3D points such as `p = (0.25, 0.25, 0.25)` against `(1,0,0), (0,1,0), (0,0,1)` (where $x+y+z = 0.75 \neq 1$) or `p = (0.25, 0.25, 0.5)` against `(0,0,0), (1,0,0), (0,1,0)`. Internal arrangement callers pre-certify coplanarity via `orient3d == 0`, so arrangement topology is unaffected, but both functions are `pub fn` crate exports with an unsound standalone contract. (`SH4.3-03`.)

## Pipeline order — Cherchi 2020 Fig. 4 / §5 & Cherchi 2022 §5 against `cherchi-rs`

Cherchi 2020 §5 (`refs/text/mesh_arrangement.txt:433-448`, Fig. 4 `:468-477`) + Cherchi 2022 §5 (`refs/text/cherchi2022_interactive_robust_mesh_booleans.txt:295-305`, Alg. 1 `:332-352`) specify:
1. Scale input coordinates by power-of-2 multiplier (`processing.cpp`).
2. Merge coincident explicit vertices and remove zero-area / duplicate input triangles (recording duplicate labels/winding for boolean restoration).
3. Build spatial acceleration structure over non-degenerate triangles and detect intersecting triangle pairs using exact explicit predicates, ignoring valid simplicial-complex neighbours (§5.1, Fig. 5b).
4. Classify each intersecting pair into explicit/LPI/jolly-LPI intersection points and symbolic constraint segments, marking coplanar overlaps (§5.1, §5.4).
5. Propagate coplanar intersection points/segments across coplanar partner triangles (§5.4).
6. For each input triangle $t \in T$ independently:
   - choose dominant-normal 2D reference projection plane (§4.2.1);
   - insert intersection points into $t$ using exact indirect predicates (§5.2);
   - enforce constraint segments via topological walk + TPI creation on segment crossings + cavity earcut (§5.3, Alg. 1);
   - if $t$ is coplanar, flood-fill sub-triangle pockets and deduplicate globally by sorted corner sequence (§5.4, Fig. 7).
7. Restore duplicate input triangles into the ray-cast substrate, partition the arrangement into manifold patches bounded by non-manifold/intersection curves, classify each patch inside/outside each input mesh via exact axis-aligned ray casting with perturbation + rational fallback (Cherchi 2022 §5), materialize surviving implicit vertices to `f64` (§5.6), and filter/orient triangles by Boolean operator.

`cherchi-rs` executes this **exact stage order** across `arrangements::soup::mesh_arrangement` (`arrangements/soup.rs:712-1135`) followed by `labeling::native::native_labeled_arrangement` (`labeling/native.rs:109-225`) and `NativeBoolean::run` (`labeling/native.rs:536-578`):
1. `compute_multiplier` + `multiply_coordinates` (`soup.rs:742-744`).
2. `merge_duplicated_vertices` (`:747`) + `remove_degenerate_and_duplicated_triangles` (`:756-757`).
3. `FastTrimesh::from_soup` (`:764`) + `detect_intersecting_pairs` (`:770`, `AabbTree` + `shared_simplex_verdict`).
4. `classify_all` (`:774`) + `deferred_pair_must_defer` (`:791-821`) + `group_intersection_points` (`:833`, geometric `PointInterner`) + `group_constraint_segments` (`:834`).
5. `integrate_coplanar_into_arrangement` (`:849`, `coplanar_propagate.rs:723`).
6. Per-base-triangle loop `for t in 0..soup.num_tris()` (`:884-1081`): `triangle_plane` (`:901`) → `split_single_triangle` (`:926`) → `enforce_constraints` (`:940`) → `find_pockets_in_triangle` + `VisitedPocketRegistry` (`:987-1042`) → `intersection_edges` harvest (`:1072-1080`).
7. Duplicate restoration (`soup.rs:1087-1114`) → `compute_all_patches` (`native.rs:117`, `patches.rs:72`) → `compute_inside_out` (`native.rs:118`, `inside_out.rs:143`) → `arrangement_mesh_with_remap` (`native.rs:133, 491`, exact `RBig` coordinate evaluation + `to_f64()`) + `lpi_through_vertex_record` (`:135, 234`) → keep-rule filtering & winding flip (`native.rs:553-577`, `labeled_arrangement.rs:132-220`).

## Unledgered divergences (ranked, exactness-first)

### 1. `U-CH-1` [CRITICAL — UNSOUND EXACTNESS FILTER] `max_component_in_triangle_normal` (`predicates/orientation.rs:55-110`) can return an axis whose exact normal component is ZERO

- **Paper (`refs/text/mesh_arrangement.txt:275-301`, App. A `:1016-1022`; `refs/text/meyer_pion2008_fpg.txt:155-189, 285-299`)**: Drops the coordinate corresponding to a provably non-zero largest component of $\vec{n} = (v_j - v_i) \times (v_k - v_i)$ so the 2D projection of $t$ is guaranteed non-degenerate ($\text{orient2d}(\Gamma(v_i), \Gamma(v_j), \Gamma(v_k)) \neq 0$). Under FPG's translation rule (`meyer_pion2008_fpg.txt:173-189`, used in Cherchi App. A `:1020-1022`), $\varepsilon_n = 8.88395 \times 10^{-16} \delta^2$ is valid **only** when $\delta = \max\{\|b-a\|_\infty, \|c-a\|_\infty\}$ (edge-difference magnitudes), with FPG underflow protection (`:285-296`). (Note: Cherchi §4.2.1 `:298-299` loosely states in prose that $\delta$ is the max of the 9 raw coordinates, which under-bounds opposite-sign edge differences by up to $2^2 = 4\times$!)
- **Code (`crates/cherchi-rs/src/predicates/orientation.rs:71-110`)**:
  1. Computes `max_var = max(|a|, |b|, |c|)` on raw vertex coordinates (`:82-96`) instead of edge differences `max(|b - a|, |c - a|)`. When $a$ and $b, c$ have opposite signs, $|b - a| \approx 2\,\text{max\_var}$, making product roundoff up to $4\times$ larger than `eps` budgets.
  2. Uses `4.0 * f64::EPSILON = 8.881784197001252e-16` (`:97`), which is *smaller* than FPG's outward-rounded `8.88395e-16` (despite the comment at `:20-24` claiming it is conservative).
  3. Tests `az > ax + eps && az > ay + eps` (`:100-108`). Because both `az` and `ax` carry roundoff error, certifying `|n_z|_exact > |n_x|_exact` requires margin $\varepsilon_z + \varepsilon_x$, and when exact `n_x, n_y` are tiny ($< \varepsilon_n$) while exact `n_z == 0`, roundoff in `nz = bx_ax * cy_ay - by_ay * cx_ax` exceeding `eps` (due to item 1) satisfies `az > ax + eps && az > ay + eps`!
  4. Omits `+ SUBNORMAL_GUARD` (`eps` underflows to `0.0` whenever `max_var < ~1.49e-154`), so subnormal rounding ($2^{-1074}$) trivially passes `az > ax + 0.0`.
- **Verified IEEE-754 $\mathcal{O}(1)$ counterexample** (exact binary `f64` inputs, $u = 2^{-53}$):
  - `a = Point3::new(-0.65625, -0.84375, 0.0)` ($-\frac{21}{32}, -\frac{27}{32}, 0$)
  - `b = Point3::new(0.65625 + 105.0 * u, 0.84375 + 135.0 * u, 0.0)`
  - `c = Point3::new(0.65625 + 63.0 * u, 0.84375 + 81.0 * u, 0.25 * u)`
  In exact arithmetic over $\mathbb{R}$, $(b_y - a_y)/(b_x - a_x) = (c_y - a_y)/(c_x - a_x) = \frac{9}{7}$, so **exact $n_z \equiv 0$**, while exact $n_x = \frac{27}{64} u + \frac{135}{4} u^2 > 0$ and exact $n_y = -\frac{21}{64} u - \frac{105}{4} u^2 \neq 0$ (`max_component_exact(a, b, c) == Axis::X`). Yet in IEEE-754 `f64`, `fl(|nz|) = 8.881784197001252e-16`, `fl(|nx|) = 4.683753385137379e-17`, `eps = 6.323050257075691e-16`, and `az > ax + eps` (`8.8818e-16 > 6.7914e-16`) is **`true`**! `max_component_in_triangle_normal(a, b, c)` returns **`Axis::Z`**, causing `soup.rs:395, 901` (`triangle_plane`) to project a non-degenerate vertical triangle onto `Plane::XY` where its three corners are **strictly collinear (zero 2D area)**, breaking `split_single_triangle` and `enforce_constraints`!
- **Call chain**: `orientation.rs:55` ← `soup.rs:396` (`triangle_plane` → `:901`), `coplanar_propagate.rs:474` (`triangle_ref_plane`), `intersection_points.rs:568, 967`, `labeling/inside_out.rs:643`.

### 4. `U-CH-4` [MEDIUM — CONTRACT / EXACTNESS DEFECT] `point_in_triangle_3d` and `point_in_triangle_3d_loc` omit 3D coplanarity (`orient3d == 0`)

- **Failure**:
  - On axis-aligned `a=(0,0,0), b=(1,0,0), c=(0,1,0)`, `p=(0.25, 0.25, 0.5)` has YZ and ZX `area2 == 0.0` (skipped) and XY `StrictlyInside`, so both functions return **`StrictlyInside`** for a point 0.5 units above the plane! (Notably, `point_in_triangle.rs:390-397` `point_off_triangle_plane` calls `let _ = point_in_triangle_3d(...)` and deliberately omits asserting `Outside`!)
  - Even on a **non-axis-aligned** triangle `a=(1,0,0), b=(0,1,0), c=(0,0,1)` (plane $x+y+z=1$), `p=(0.25, 0.25, 0.25)` ($x+y+z = 0.75 \neq 1$, off-plane!) projects strictly inside `(0,0)-(1,0)-(0,1)` on **all three** coordinate planes (`0.25 > 0` and `0.25 + 0.25 = 0.5 < 1`), so both functions return **`StrictlyInside`** even when none of the three projections is degenerate!
- **Impact**: Internal `cherchi-rs` arrangement callers (`intersection_points.rs:403, 757`, `aux_structure.rs:501`) guard coplanarity via `orient3d == 0` beforehand, so arrangement output is safe, but any external caller using the `pub fn` API (`cherchi_rs::predicates::{point_in_triangle_3d, point_in_triangle_3d_loc}`) gets false positives on off-plane points.

### 5. `U-CH-5` [MEDIUM — MISSING PAPER MECHANISM] Missing §5.4 coplanar TPI fallback (`computeTriangleOfSegmentInCoplanarCase`) + coplanar `source_tri` in `coplanar_propagate.rs:807-839`

- **Paper (`refs/text/mesh_arrangement.txt:530-537`, §5.3 + §5.4 `:559-570`)**: When two constraint segments cross inside a triangle $t$ and their supporting planes are not linearly independent with $t$ (coplanar overlap), the new vertex is constructed using §5.4's auxiliary tetrahedron (`jolly_point` paired with a segment to form a non-coplanar plane).
- **Code (`arrangements/coplanar_propagate.rs:807-839` → `arrangements/enforce.rs:461, 525-535`)**: `coplanar_propagate.rs:818-838` sets `ConstraintSegment.source_tri = src` where `src` is a **coplanar partner triangle** (normal parallel to `base`!). Comment `:809-812` assumes *"the segment can only meet other in-plane constraints at shared endpoints, a V-junction, so no spurious TPI arises"*. That assumption fails whenever:
  1. Three or more coplanar triangles overlap in a soup (e.g., variadic booleans or general soup arrangement) so two coplanar partner edges cross inside a third coplanar triangle $t$; or
  2. A transversal triangle $t_{\text{trans}}$ slices across $t$ and crosses a coplanar partner's overlap segment inside $t$ (`base` and `item.source_tri` or `plane_e` are coplanar, so `det(n_base, n_coplanar, n_trans) == 0`!).
  In both cases `create_tpi` (`enforce.rs:525-535`) returns `Err(EnforceError::DegenerateTpi)` → `ArrangementError::DeepRecursionRequired` (`soup.rs:967`).

### 6. `U-CH-6` [LOW-MEDIUM — DEFEATED PAPER ACCELERATORS] Unused `Tree` (`arrangements/tree.rs`) and per-call `GenericPoint3D` cache destruction (`arrangements/gp_dispatch.rs:34-41`)

- **Paper**: Cherchi 2020 §5.2 (`mesh_arrangement.txt:501-511`) uses a per-triangle 2/3-ary `Tree` to locate sub-triangles during point insertion, and Attene §5.4 (`attene-predicates.txt:328-341`) caches `LambdaF64` (`l, d, beta`) and `LambdaIv` per implicit point across predicate calls.

  2. `gp_dispatch.rs:34-41` `to_generic(subm.vert_coords(v))` allocates a brand-new `GenericPoint3D` (with empty `OnceLock`s) on every triangle/ear/edge check in `retriangulate.rs:102-120` and `enforce.rs:690-693, 782-804`, and `Clone for GenericPoint3D` (`indirect/mod.rs:239-248`) discards populated `OnceLock`s. Storing `GenericPoint3D` directly on `FastTrimesh` submesh vertices (as `inside_out.rs:1177` does for ray sorting) would preserve Attene §5.4's cache across all $\mathcal{O}(k)$ predicate calls per vertex.

### 7. `U-CH-7` [LOW — UNLEDGERED SUBSTITUTION + SILENT HOLE EXIT] `earcut_linear` (`arrangements/enforce.rs:670-745`)

- **Paper**: Cherchi 2020 §5.3 (`mesh_arrangement.txt:571-573, 599`) uses $O(n^2)$ general earcut; Cherchi 2022 §4 (`cherchi2022_interactive_robust_mesh_booleans.txt:332-336`) substitutes Livesu et al. 2021 deterministic $O(n)$ `earcutLinear`.

### 8. `U-CH-8` [LOW — STAGE-3 ARITHMETIC & INTERVAL EMULATION TRADE-OFFS] `dashu::RBig` Stage-3 & `next_down`/`next_up` Stage-2 (`predicates/indirect/`)

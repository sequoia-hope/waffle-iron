## N27 — Stage-1 rim-override merge onto a coinciding uniform sample (`docs/yang_deviations.md:2170-2229`)

1. **Deviation & paper departure.** Stage-1 boundary discretization bit-replaces an interior uniform angular Steiner sample with a Stage-0/junction override point whenever their parameter gap falls below `uni_step * 1e-6` and their 3D distance is `< TAU_MODEL` (`1e-7`), preserving uniform ring length so structured `(N - k)` lateral index-pairing avoids UV-CDT. Then, for each boundary curve, apply constrained Delaunay triangulation (CDT) in CGAL [2024] to retriangulate the two adjacent surfaces around the boundary"*.

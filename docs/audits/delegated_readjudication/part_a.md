## D14 — Analytic 5-surface B-Rep scope instead of NURBS / rational Bézier B-Rep

1. **Deviation & paper departure:** Waffle Iron represents B-Rep faces and edges exclusively with five closed-form analytic surface types (`Plane`, `Sphere`, `Cylinder`, `Cone`, `Torus`) and conic/procedural intersection curves rather than trimmed NURBS / rational Bézier patches and fitted B-spline curves.
   - Yang et al. 2025 §4.1.1 (`refs/text/yang2025_hybrid_boolean.txt:304-311`): *"We convert the surface patches of the B-Rep models into NURBS surfaces as inputs of this step... Assuming that a NURBS surface $\mathbf{p}(u,v)$ is defined on $u \in [u_0, u_1], v \in [v_0, v_1]$, we note that the NURBS surface can be partitioned into a collection of rational Bézier sub-patches [Piegl and Tiller 1996]..."*
   - Yang et al. 2025 §5 (`refs/text/yang2025_hybrid_boolean.txt:740-741`): *"Our algorithm can work on all watertight manifold B-Rep models, regardless of the types of surfaces involved."*
2. **Exists at HEAD (`b4bee54e`)?** **Yes** (with a stale ledger path). The ledger entry (`docs/yang_deviations.md:3775`) cites `crates/kernel/src/geometry/surface.rs`, which was deleted in commit `5bd4627d` (`Stage 4b — DELETE crates/kernel`). At HEAD the 5-surface analytic scope lives in `crates/yang-rs/src/geom.rs:37-72` (`pub enum Surface { Plane, Sphere, Cylinder, Cone, Torus }`), `crates/yang-rs/src/geom.rs:85-136` (`pub enum Curve { LineSegment, Circle, Ellipse, Parabola, Hyperbola, SurfacePair }`), and `crates/kernel-v2/src/arena.rs:245-346` (`pub enum Surface`, same 5 analytic variants; no NURBS/B-spline surface or p-curve variant exists).
3. **Roadmap milestone:** **F7** (`docs/yang_faithfulness_roadmap.md:201`: *"D14 + N94 as one analytic-only scope signature"*). Milestones **F2**, **F4**, and **F5** implement the paper's general $(u,v)$ parameter-domain CDT and surface-evaluator operations over the 5 analytic types so NURBS would later be an additive `Surface` variant rather than a pipeline rewrite, without adding NURBS itself.
4. **Known customers:** Workspace-wide scope boundary: every `kernel-v2`, `yang-rs`, `ssi-rs`, and `step-import` entrypoint (`crates/kernel-v2/src/boolean/to_yang.rs`, all 374 corpus cases in `app/tests/cases/assay/`). Freeform STEP entities are rejected at import rather than lowered to NURBS.
5. **Recommendation:** **ASK** — Yang et al. 2025 §4.1.1 (`refs/text/yang2025_hybrid_boolean.txt:304-311`) and §5 (`:740-741`) formulate the hybrid Boolean pipeline over arbitrary trimmed NURBS / rational Bézier B-Rep surfaces, whereas Waffle Iron scopes `yang_rs::Surface` (`crates/yang-rs/src/geom.rs:37-72`) and `kernel_v2::Surface` (`crates/kernel-v2/src/arena.rs:245-346`) to the five analytic CAD primitives (`Plane`, `Cylinder`, `Cone`, `Sphere`, `Torus`) plus exact algebraic/procedural SSI per governance A15.1 (`D14` paired with `N94`).

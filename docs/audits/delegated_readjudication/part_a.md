## D14 — Analytic 5-surface B-Rep scope instead of NURBS / rational Bézier B-Rep

1. **Deviation & paper departure:** Waffle Iron represents B-Rep faces and edges exclusively with five closed-form analytic surface types (`Plane`, `Sphere`, `Cylinder`, `Cone`, `Torus`) and conic/procedural intersection curves rather than trimmed NURBS / rational Bézier patches and fitted B-spline curves.
   - Yang et al. 2025 §4.1.1 (`refs/text/yang2025_hybrid_boolean.txt:304-311`): *"We convert the surface patches of the B-Rep models into NURBS surfaces as inputs of this step... Assuming that a NURBS surface $\mathbf{p}(u,v)$ is defined on $u \in [u_0, u_1], v \in [v_0, v_1]$, we note that the NURBS surface can be partitioned into a collection of rational Bézier sub-patches [Piegl and Tiller 1996]..."*
   - Yang et al. 2025 §5 (`refs/text/yang2025_hybrid_boolean.txt:740-741`): *"Our algorithm can work on all watertight manifold B-Rep models, regardless of the types of surfaces involved."*
2. **Exists at HEAD (`1f946a51`)?** **Yes in code, and already reopened from `DELEGATED` to `OPEN` on `main` (2026-10-27).** At HEAD the 5-surface analytic representation lives in `crates/yang-rs/src/geom.rs:37-72` (`pub enum Surface { Plane, Sphere, Cylinder, Cone, Torus }`), `crates/yang-rs/src/geom.rs:85-136` (`pub enum Curve`), and `crates/kernel-v2/src/arena.rs:245-346` (`pub enum Surface`). On 2026-10-27 (`cc2d4f30`), the user reopened `D14` (`docs/yang_deviations.md:3769-3791`: *"Decision (user, 2026-10-27): REOPENED — analytic-only is not the end state... State: OPEN"*). (Note: `docs/yang_deviations.md:3793` still retains historical `crates/kernel/src/geometry/surface.rs:56-62` text alongside its deletion note, and `:3795` cites `yang2025:330-395` for §4.1.1 NURBS-to-Bézier conversion instead of `:304-322`.)
3. **Roadmap milestone:** **F5** (`docs/yang_faithfulness_roadmap.md:171-202, 218-220`). F5 introduces a generic parametric-surface interface that every stage reaches surfaces through (with the five analytic primitives as instances and closed-form SSI under `N7` kept as the exact path alongside general Newton), builds the one-CDT-per-face Stage 1 on that interface, and carries a first non-analytic instance (ruled / B-spline surface, P&T Ch. 10; `specs/loft.md`) end to end. `N94`'s former scope pairing with `D14` has lapsed (`N94`'s p-curves are re-adjudicated with F5's interface, `:219-220`).

## N5 — Per-surface Stage-1 analytic tessellators instead of §4.1 recursive $(u,v)$ quadtree discretization

1. **Deviation & paper departure:** Stage 1 dispatches by `Surface` variant to bespoke per-primitive tessellators (planar fan/CDT, uniform cylinder tube, cone frustum, sphere patch/chart, torus structured band/chart) rather than one uniform $(u,v)$ domain subdivision loop certified by $d_\epsilon$ and $d(T)$.
   - Yang et al. 2025 §4.1.1 (`refs/text/yang2025_hybrid_boolean.txt:316-321`): *"For each sub-patch $\mathbf{p}(u,v)$ with control points $\mathbf{P} = \mathbf{p}_{i,j}$ where $i = 0, \dots, m, j = 0, \dots, n$, we create two triangles from the four corner control points, i.e. $\triangle\mathbf{p}_{00}\mathbf{p}_{0n}\mathbf{p}_{m0}$ and $\triangle\mathbf{p}_{0n}\mathbf{p}_{m0}\mathbf{p}_{mn}$, as an initial discretization of the surface. If the distance from the Bézier patch to these two triangles exceeds $d_\epsilon$, it is subdivided into four sub-patches until the tolerance is fulfilled, while the mesh is updated throughout."*
   - Yang et al. 2025 §4.1.2 (`refs/text/yang2025_hybrid_boolean.txt:404-407` + `:350-351`): *"For each surface, we first triangulate the rectangular u-v domain until reaching the given distance tolerance $d_\epsilon$. Then, for each boundary curve, apply constrained Delaunay triangulation (CDT) in CGAL [2024] to retriangulate the two adjacent surfaces around the boundary, and remove the trimmed area as in [Diazzi et al. 2023], if it’s a trimmed surface, generating a watertight mesh."*
2. **Exists at HEAD (`1f946a51`)?** **Yes** (with drifted ledger line numbers). The ledger entry (`docs/yang_deviations.md:1153`) cites `crates/yang-rs/src/stage1_tessellate.rs:2183-2223` (planar fan only). At HEAD `stage1_tessellate_once` (`crates/yang-rs/src/stage1_tessellate.rs:2360-2634`) dispatches across surface-specific builders: planar all-line fan/CDT (`:2361-2460`, `:2682-3123`), planar `tessellate_cap_face` (`:2461-2516`, `:3130-3211`), cylinder `tessellate_lateral_face` (`:2517-2550`, `:4728`), sphere `tessellate_sphere_face` (`crates/yang-rs/src/stage1_tessellate/surface_face_tessellators.rs:25`), cone `tessellate_cone_face` (`surface_face_tessellators.rs:208`), and torus `tessellate_torus_face` (`crates/yang-rs/src/stage1_tessellate/patch_tessellators.rs:47`), plus cross-patch angular folds (`N88`) and `self_contact.rs`.
3. **Roadmap milestone:** **F5** (`docs/yang_faithfulness_roadmap.md:171-202`: *"F5 — Stage 1 as §4.1 on a generic surface ... One CDT per face, replacing the per-surface dispatch (tube, frustum, chart, universal cover, azimuth zipper), with cones included (N61), built on that interface... ## N11 — Sphere section-circle `(R / r_circle) · d_ε` radial chord-band amplification in Stage 3 & Stage 4

   - Yang et al. 2025 §4.3 (`refs/text/yang2025_hybrid_boolean.txt:528-537`): *"We use a numeric optimization method to obtain more accurate intersection curves and distinguish tangent points and small loops... If the distance between the two points converges to zero during the optimization process, an accurate intersection point is found that lies on both $S_A$ and $S_B$, as shown in Fig. 10 (c)."*
